Questions we get before the first call

How fast is an episode?

Five business days from the day your material arrives to the published episode, on every tier. Send a full month of material at once and the batch still releases on the dates you choose.

How many revisions?

Two rounds per episode, on every tier. One on the script before production begins, one on the finished audio after delivery. Both are inside the retainer.

Who owns the content?

You do. Scripts, audio files, transcripts, clips, and written versions are yours on delivery, and the show lives in your own hosting account under your name.

What if my source material is messy?

That is the normal starting point. A rough transcript, an unfinished draft, or a recorded webinar is enough to build from. When one piece is too thin for a full episode, we combine two or three, and you approve the direction at the script round.
